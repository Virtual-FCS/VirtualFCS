VirtualFCS.Fluid.PSAInterface

This is a generic interface for a pressure swing adsorption unit, with an inlet port_a and two outlet ports port_b{1,2}) and 3 mediums (Medium_{1,2,3}).
The ports are assigned to different mediums although ideally a single mixture medium could/should be used when extended. This approach is chosen to give flexibility in case e.g. the outlet port needs to be assigned to a single-phase medium for use in downstream models.
