VirtualFCS.Media.PEMELfluid

Generic fluid on anode and cathode of a PEM electrolyser

This medium is an extension of MoistAir in which also hydrogen is included in the gas phase.
This allows this medium to model hydrogen contamination and cross-over through PEM membranes.
