VirtualFCS.SubSystems.Hydrogen.SubSystemHydrogen

Model Description and References

SubSystemHydrogen supplies hydrogen to a fuel-cell anode and manages recirculation, pressure reduction, and purge flow.

Composition

The assembly contains a 0.13 m3 hydrogen tank, pressure regulator, recirculation blower, purge valve, mass-flow and temperature sensors, and a fixed-temperature tank heat-transfer model. Hydrogen enters the stack through toStack and returns through fromStack; pin_p and pin_n supply the blower and control hardware.

Working principle

Stack current is converted to hydrogen consumption and passed to SubSystemHydrogenControl. The control block sets feed pressure, recirculation-blower speed, and purge-valve opening. The tank starts at 35 MPa, while exhaust hydrogen is discharged to the system ambient pressure.

The anode medium, cell count, recirculation ratio, tank area, and minimum blower flow are configurable. Pressure-control tuning and purge settings must be checked for the connected stack.
