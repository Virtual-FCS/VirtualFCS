Differential of the Rachford-Rice relation for flash equilibria:
h(β) = Σi zi/(iKi+β(1-iKi) = 0

Having defined the convenience variables:
Di=iKi+β(1-iKi)
S = Σi (1-iKi)2zi/Di

The differential is then dβ = Σi(1-iKi)/(S*Di) dzi - zi/(S*Di2) diKi
