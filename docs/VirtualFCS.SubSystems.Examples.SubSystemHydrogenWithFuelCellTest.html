VirtualFCS.SubSystems.Examples.SubSystemHydrogenWithFuelCellTest

Model Description and References

This test couples the hydrogen subsystem to a 42-cell fuel cell. The fuel-cell load resistance steps from -8 ohm to 2 ohm at 250 s.

The air feed is 0.075 kg/s with a 2e5 Pa outlet, the anode starts at 1e5 Pa, and coolant enters at 323.15 K and 1 kg/s. The hydrogen subsystem is supplied at 24 V and uses a proportional pressure regulator.
