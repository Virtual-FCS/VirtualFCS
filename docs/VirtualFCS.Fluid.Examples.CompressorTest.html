VirtualFCS.Fluid.Examples.CompressorTest

Model Description and References

This test verifies the operation of a Compressor object in combination with an applied constant torque applied on its shaft, a mixing volume with mass and energy hold-up at the outlet, and an orifice leading air back to the environment to realise a pressure drop.

Note that the compressor is using its heatPort to simulate adiabatic operation.

For correct initialisation, the pressure of the compressor outlet (i.e. the mixing volume) is set to 1% higher than the environment's at start; this avoid spurious solutions in the calculation of the compressor.
