VirtualFCS.Control.ECMS.EMS_ECMS

Model Description and References

The EnergyManagementSystem component is designed to manage the flow of power between the fuel cell stack, battery, vehicle load, and balance-of-plant load. It splits the load according to pre-defined energy management rules, which are implemented within the bounds of the battery management system and the fuel cell control unit.

Despite its name, controlSetCurrent carries the fuel-cell power request in W: it is driven by set_FC_power_3 through the slew-rate limiter and absolute-value block. The connected FuelCellSystemController converts that power to current. The current-estimation equation calls fcVoltage with a fixed 42-cell stack; changing n_cell does not change that literal. Battery current is sign-reversed internally, and measured battery voltage is bounded below by 1 V.
