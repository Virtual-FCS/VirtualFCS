VirtualFCS.Fluid.Separator

A lumped fixed-volume gas/liquid separator

Model Description and References

This unit separates the phases of a condensable medium flow. It can operate at fixed temperature (heatPort=false), adiabatically (heatPort=true) or with heat dissipation models with appropriate units connected to the heat port.

Note that all ports have the same pressure, and to achieve physically meaningful results it will most often be necessary to connect them to a valve or other flow resistance unit before flushing to a constant-pressure reservoir.
Two output signals for level and pressure are provided, since these are the values that typically control the output flows.

In case of reverse flow, the feed port's (mid_port) height can be adjusted with feedHeight to set whether it will output liquid or gas depending on the liquid level in the separator.
If the separator runs out of liquid or overflows, the compositions and enthalpy of the liquid viz. the gas outlet will eventually change to the other phase; the transition interval depend on the pipeHeight parameter.

Governing Equations

V_gas = m*medium.beta/medium.d_gas;

V_liq = m*(1 - medium.beta)/medium.d_liq;

level = V_liq/V;

port_top.Xi_outflow = smoothStep(V_gas/V - pipeHeight, medium.X_gas[1:Medium.nXi], medium.X_liq[1:Medium.nXi], pipeHeight);

port_mid.Xi_outflow = smoothStep(V_liq/V - feedHeight, port_top.Xi_outflow, port_bottom.Xi_outflow, pipeHeight);

port_bottom.Xi_outflow = smoothStep(V_liq/V - pipeHeight, medium.X_liq[1:Medium.nXi], medium.X_gas[1:Medium.nXi], pipeHeight);

port_top.h_outflow = smoothStep(V_gas/V - pipeHeight, medium.h_gas, medium.h_liq, pipeHeight);

port_mid.h_outflow = smoothStep(V_liq/V - feedHeight, port_top.h_outflow, port_bottom.h_outflow, pipeHeight);

port_bottom.h_outflow = smoothStep(V_liq/V - pipeHeight, medium.h_liq, medium.h_gas, pipeHeight);
