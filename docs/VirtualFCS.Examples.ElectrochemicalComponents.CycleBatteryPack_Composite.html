VirtualFCS.Examples.ElectrochemicalComponents.CycleBatteryPack_Composite

Example demonstrating constant-current constant-voltage cycling of a composite Li-ion battery pack model.

Model Description and References

This example cycles a composite Li-ion pack containing three parallel strings of three series cells (3p3s). A pulse current alternates between -3.3 A and +3.3 A over a 7200 s experiment.

The pack starts at ambient temperature, uses a lower BMS voltage limit of 9 V, and exposes each cell individually. Use the lumped-pack example when cell-level resolution is not required.
