Activation-loss model for the anode and cathode of a hydrogen fuel cell.

Governing Equations

j = i/A_cell;

E_act_A = R*T_stack/(a_A*F)*Modelica.Math.asinh(j/2*i_0);

E_act = E_act_A + E_act_C;

v = E_act;
