Overview
VirtualFCS is an open Modelica library for dynamic modelling of hydrogen and hybrid energy systems. It brings together electrochemical, electrical, fluid, thermal, control, and vehicle models, with examples ranging from PEM fuel cell vehicles to ammonia cracking. The library provides classes describing:
  • Electrochemical systems: PEM fuel cells and stacks, fuel-cell systems, batteries, and PEM electrolysers.
  • Balance of plant: hydrogen, air, and cooling subsystems, compressors, humidifiers, heat exchangers, and related controls.
  • System integration: battery-electric and fuel cell range-extender powertrains.
  • Other applications: ammonia-cracking system case study, vehicle drive cycles, hardware-, software-, and user-in-the-loop examples.
The VirtualFCS library is composed of 8 main sub-packages: Examples, Chemical, Control, Electrical, Electrochemical, Fluid, Media, Thermal, Powertrains, Subsystems, Vehicles, and Utilities.
