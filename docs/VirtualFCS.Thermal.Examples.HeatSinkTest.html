VirtualFCS.Thermal.Examples.HeatSinkTest

Simple model to test the HeatSink model

Model Description and References

This test applies a 0.01 Hz sine control to the coolant pump from 10 s, with amplitude 0.14 and offset 0.15. The coolant inlet is initially 100 K above the default temperature and the thermal setpoint is 350 K.

The battery starts at SOC = 0.9 with 23 V to 27 V limits and 25 V nominal voltage.
