VirtualFCS.Electrical.Examples.BoostConverterTest

Model Description and References

This test drives the detailed two-phase boost converter with a 10 Hz sinusoidal duty command of amplitude 0.4 and offset 0.5, while the switching PWM runs at 100 kHz.

The electrical test bench uses a 100 V source, 10 ohm load, 0.1 ohm internal resistance, 20 uF input capacitor, and a 323.15 K thermal boundary.
