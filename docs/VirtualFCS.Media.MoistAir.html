VirtualFCS.Media.MoistAir

Medium model for cathodic air in PEM fuel cells

This medium models air in fuel cells; it can also be used for e.g. the anodic side in PEM electrolysers.

The medium includes separate accounts of oxygen and nitrogen, in addition to tracking humidity and condensation.

Note that the class reimplements flashVaporisation with a direct formula to exploit the fact that only water is considered condensable.
