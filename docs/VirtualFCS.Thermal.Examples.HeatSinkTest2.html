VirtualFCS.Thermal.Examples.HeatSinkTest2

Simple model to test the HeatSink model

Model Description and References

This alternative heat-sink test applies periodic trapezoids: coolant temperature varies from 293.15 K to 373.15 K and coolant flow from 0 to 0.2 kg/s. Both signals use 130 s rise/fall times and a 300 s period.

The battery starts at SOC = 0.9 with 59 V to 61 V limits and 60 V nominal voltage.
