VirtualFCS.Examples.ElectrochemicalComponents.PolarizeFuelCellStack

Generate a polarization curve for a fuel cell stack.

Model Description and References

This example generates a reference fuel-cell polarization curve by stepping the electrical load through 20 A at 0 s, 150 A at 50 s, and 370 A at 100 s.

The stack has 180 cells and is connected to hydrogen, air, and cooling subsystems. The nominal operating conditions are 2e5 Pa on the reactant sides, 333.15 K coolant, air lambda = 1.7, and hydrogen recirculation ratio = 1.3.

Governing Equations

The assembly connects system (Modelica.Fluid.System), fcs (Electrochemical.Hydrogen.FuelCells.FuelCellSystem), resistor (Modelica.Electrical.Analog.Basic.VariableResistor), ground (Modelica.Electrical.Analog.Basic.Ground), Resistance (Modelica.Blocks.Sources.RealExpression), I_aprox (Modelica.Blocks.Sources.CombiTimeTable).
