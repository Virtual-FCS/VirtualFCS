VirtualFCS.Fluid.Humidifier

Discretised hollow-fiber membrane humidifier with fluid holdup

Model Description and References

This model implements the Pollak et al. hollow-fiber membrane humidifier as two counter-current trains of n discretised fluid volumes (wetVolume on the fiber side and dryVolume on the shell side). Each paired cell exchanges water vapor through a MembraneTransfer block and heat through a ThermalConductor. The gas volumes provide mass, species, and energy holdup.


Positive transfer[i].m_transfer moves water from the wet train to the dry train. The signed transfer, its carried enthalpy, and its composition are connected to both cell volumes through their membrane_m_flow, membrane_h_outflow, and membrane_Xi inputs, where they enter the standard mass, species, and enthalpy balances implemented by MembraneManifold. Port outlet states are therefore computed by the cell media rather than assigned directly by the humidifier.

Assumptions

  • Gas-phase water is the dependent medium species, selected by waterIndex.
  • The wet train flows from portA_in to portA_out and the dry train counter-currently from portB_in to portB_out.
  • Pressure drop is represented by each cell's flow resistance, sized from dp_nominal and m_flow_nom.
  • The Pollak Sherwood correlations and membrane resistance are applied locally to each axial cell by the instantiated MembraneTransfer components.

Each MembraneTransfer component filters its wet-side and dry-side flow signals with time constant tau_flow. m_flow_start initializes the wet filter to a positive flow and the counter-current dry filter to its negative. Gas volumes retain the mass, composition, and energy holdup; no additional first-order permeation-rate state is used. The shell geometry dHousing is passed to every transfer cell.

References

Reference: Pollak et al., Steady state and dynamic simulation of a small-scale hollow fiber membrane humidifier.
