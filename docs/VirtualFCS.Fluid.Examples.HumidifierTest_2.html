VirtualFCS.Fluid.Examples.HumidifierTest_2

Model Description and References

This alternative humidifier test uses slow sinusoidal excitation: wet flow has 0.0004 kg/s offset and 0.00003 kg/s amplitude at 0.05 mHz; temperature has 343 K offset and 10 K amplitude at 0.04 mHz; humidity has 0.75 offset and 0.25 amplitude at 0.03 mHz.

The dry feed is 0.0004 kg/s at 353.15 K with X = {0.24, 0.76, 0}; outlet pressures are 2e5 Pa and 1.8e5 Pa.

Governing Equations

X_mol[Water] = humidity.y * Modelica.Media.Air.ReferenceMoistAir.Utilities.Water95_Utilities.psat(temperature.y)/wet_sink.p;

X_mol[Oxygen] = 0.21 * (1-X_mol[Water]);

X_mol[Nitrogen] = 0.79 * (1-X_mol[Water]);

wet_source.X_in = MMX .* X_mol / (MMX*X_mol);
