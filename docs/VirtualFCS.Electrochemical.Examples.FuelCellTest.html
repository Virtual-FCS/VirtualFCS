VirtualFCS.Electrochemical.Examples.FuelCellTest

Model Description and References

This test applies a load step from 3 ohm to 0.6 ohm at 30 s to a 42-cell fuel cell. The hydrogen inlet is 3e5 Pa, the air outlet is 2.5e5 Pa, and the air flow is 0.003 kg/s.

The glycol coolant enters at 323.15 K with a flow of 0.05 kg/s.
