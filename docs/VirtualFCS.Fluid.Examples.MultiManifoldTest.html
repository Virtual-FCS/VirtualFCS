VirtualFCS.Fluid.Examples.MultiManifoldTest

Model Description and References

This test connects anode and cathode manifolds with reaction-derived flows. The reaction rate steps from 0.1 to 1 at 0.5 s; lambda = 3 and cell voltage = 0.7 V determine the resulting flow rates.

Both manifold volumes are 0.01 m3 and share a heat port.
