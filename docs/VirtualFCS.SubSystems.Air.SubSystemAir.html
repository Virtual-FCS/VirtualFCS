VirtualFCS.SubSystems.Air.SubSystemAir

Subsystem regulating pressure and flowrate of air to a fuel cell stack

Model Description and References

SubSystemAir supplies cathode air to a fuel-cell stack and regulates the stack-side pressure.

Composition

The subsystem contains an electric compressor, a pressure sensor, an outlet valve, a pressure set-point source, and PI control. The compressor is powered through the electrical pins; its outlet feeds the stack through toStack, while fromStack returns the cathode flow to the environment.

Working principle

The stack-current input is converted to an estimated air mass-flow demand using the cell count and air excess ratio lambda. Compressor control supplies the required flow, while the valve and pressure controller regulate the cathode pressure toward p_set. The environment boundary uses the global system ambient pressure and temperature.

The compressor and valve are replaceable. Controller gains and nominal flow values are tuned for a particular stack and should be checked when changing stack size, manifold volume, or operating conditions.
