VirtualFCS.Chemical.AmmoniaCracker.AmmoniaCrackerDyn

Dynamic (lumped-volume per segment) ammonia cracker. Same kinetics as AmmoniaCracker, but each of the N computational segments is a Modelica.Fluid.Interfaces.PartialLumpedVolume-style control volume so that transient pressure build-up is captured.

2. Model description

AmmoniaCrackerDyn is a segmented catalytic ammonia-decomposition reactor for system-level transient studies. It represents the conversion of ammonia into hydrogen and nitrogen, gas accumulation, pressure-driven transport, and thermal response of the gas and tube walls. Applications include studying feed changes, outlet throttling, and coupling to distributed heaters, gas separation, and hydrogen-consuming equipment.

Each of the N segments contains separate NH3, H2, and N2 mass inventories. Ideal-gas partial pressures determine segment pressure. Adjacent segments exchange mass through regularized quadratic pressure-loss laws, with half-segment friction at the inlet and outlet and optional additional header losses. Face compositions use a smoothed donor-cell formulation for forward and reverse flow. The friction factor is fixed at its nominal-flow value; the locally calculated Reynolds number is a diagnostic, not a friction feedback.

A dynamic temperature balance is solved in every segment. Gas and tube wall share one temperature and a combined heat capacity. Heat supplied through heatPorts balances inflow thermal transport, endothermic reaction demand, and heat exchange with ambient. The forward inlet term uses the incoming stream enthalpy and the enthalpy of the feed composition at segment conditions. Internal transport uses a specific-heat approximation; at the outlet, reverse thermal inflow is approximated at the last segment temperature rather than using the external outlet stream enthalpy.

Species inventories have fixed initial values calculated from p_start, T_start, and X_start. Temperature start values are not fixed, so the surrounding model must provide suitable initialization conditions. T_out_target is used to evaluate k_eq and is not a temperature constraint or an internal controller set-point.

3. Governing equations and references

The reaction stoichiometry per mole of ammonia consumed is NH3 -> 1.5 H2 + 0.5 N2. Representative segment equations are:

m_tot[i] = m_NH3[i] + m_H2[i] + m_N2[i]

P_tot[i] = (n_NH3[i] + n_H2[i] + n_N2[i])*R*T[i]/V_seg

der(m_species[i]) = inlet species flow - outlet species flow

                    + nu_species*R_NH3[i]*MM_species

r_rxn[i] = max(0, min(rate(...), n_NH3[i]/(tau_rxn_min*m_cat_seg)))

R_NH3[i] = r_rxn[i]*m_cat_seg

Q_rxn[i] = deltaH_rxn*R_NH3[i]

Q_loss[i] = U_tot*A_s/N*(T[i] - T_a)

(m_tot[i]*cp_mix[i] + C_solid_seg)*der(T[i])

  = inflow thermal transport - Q_loss[i] - Q_rxn[i] + heatPorts[i].Q_flow

m_flow_face[j] = regRoot(upstream pressure - downstream pressure, dp_reg)

                 /sqrt(k_face[j])

dp_reg = dp_small at the inlet and outlet faces, dp_small_internal at the internal faces

The quadratic friction between neighbouring segments is very small, so the physical internal pressure drops are much smaller than the absolute tolerance of the segment pressures. Without additional regularization, the internal face flows become dominated by solver noise at low feed flow, causing very small step sizes. The default dp_small_internal = 1e8 Pa makes these faces effectively linear. This has a negligible effect on the overall pressure profile and outlet conditions.

The kinetic expression follows Realpe et al. (2023), doi:10.1039/d2re00408a, with activation energy Ea = 1.54e5 J/mol and exponents a = 0.37 and b = -1.04 for the Co-Ba/CeO2 catalyst. Partial pressures are divided by 1e5 in the rate expression; NH3 and H2 pressure ratios are floored at 1e-6. Negative net kinetic rates are clipped to zero, so the model does not implement reverse ammonia synthesis. The inventory-based upper limit restricts ammonia consumption on the timescale tau_rxn_min.

As implemented, r_rxn is interpreted as mol/(s.g_cat) and multiplied by m_cat_seg in grams. The k0 declaration describes its units as mol/(kg.s); this convention should be checked when changing or calibrating catalyst data. Species balances use SI molar masses MM_NH3 = 0.017, MM_H2 = 0.002, and MM_N2 = 0.028 kg/mol.

The nominal friction factor uses 64/Re for laminar flow, a linear blend between Re = 2300 and 4000, and 0.3163*Re^(-0.25) above 4000. The function floors Re at 1e-3. Positive segment volumes, flow areas, nominal flow, regularization scales, and physically consistent initial compositions are required.

The model follows a lumped-inventory approach similar to Modelica.Fluid.Interfaces.PartialLumpedVolume, but implements its own species and approximate temperature balances rather than extending that class. Medium properties are supplied by CrackedAmmonia by default.
