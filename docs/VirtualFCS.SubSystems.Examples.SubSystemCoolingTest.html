VirtualFCS.SubSystems.Examples.SubSystemCoolingTest

Simple model to test the compressor component

Model Description and References

This test steps the applied heat flow from 0 W to 6000 W at 10 s. The coolant system starts at 300 K and 101325 Pa with 0.1 kg/s flow; the pipe starts at 363.15 K and uses 1 m length and 0.01 m diameter.

The electrical supply is 24 V and the stack PI controller has Ti = 5.
