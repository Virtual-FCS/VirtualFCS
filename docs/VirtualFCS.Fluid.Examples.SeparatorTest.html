VirtualFCS.Fluid.Examples.SeparatorTest

Model Description and References

This example implements a separator layout with level and pressure control.
The input is initially 50% water in dry air, allowing the separator to fill, after which only dry air is fed, evaporating the water and emptying the separator again.
