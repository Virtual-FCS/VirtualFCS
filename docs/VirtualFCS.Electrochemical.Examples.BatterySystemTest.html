VirtualFCS.Electrochemical.Examples.BatterySystemTest

Model Description and References

This test applies a battery power demand of 25.8 kW from 0 to 10000 s, then steps it to -26.2 kW at 10000 s. The battery starts at SOC = 0.99.

The electrical source is connected directly to the battery system; battery electrical and thermal parameters otherwise use their model defaults.
