VirtualFCS.SubSystems.Examples.SubSystemAirWithFuelCellTest

Model Description and References

This test couples the air subsystem to a 42-cell fuel cell with 0.075 kg/s air feed, 1 kg/s coolant, 323.15 K coolant inlet, and a 24 V subsystem supply.

A load-resistance step from 30 ohm to 50 ohm is scheduled at 250 s.
