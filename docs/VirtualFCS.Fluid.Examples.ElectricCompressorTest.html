VirtualFCS.Fluid.Examples.ElectricCompressorTest

Model Description and References

This test starts the compressor at 0.001 kg/s and steps the commanded mass flow to 0.301 kg/s at 10 s.

The bench uses a 48 V supply, a 2e5 Pa pressure setpoint, a 2.5e5 Pa nominal valve pressure, and a 1 m3 mixing volume at 353.15 K. The nominal flow is 0.3 kg/s with a 1.5e5 Pa nominal pressure difference.
