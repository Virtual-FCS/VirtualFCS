VirtualFCS.Control.ECMS.costFunction

Model Description and References

Evaluates the ECMS equivalent hydrogen-consumption rate in g/s for a candidate fuel-cell/battery power split. Fuel-cell consumption is estimated from the lower heating value and the empirical efficiencyFC function. The battery contribution is weighted by sigma and corrected by the battery state of charge through mu. The implementation uses SOC_max + SOC_min in the state-of-charge normalization denominator. Positive battery power contributes a positive equivalent-consumption term.

Governing Equations

cost := ((powerFC)/(LHVH2 * efficiencyFC(powerFC))) + ((1-2*mu*((SOC - 0.5*(SOC_min+SOC_max))/(SOC_max+SOC_min))) * ((sigma * powerBatt)/(LHVH2 * efficiencyFC(powerFC))));
