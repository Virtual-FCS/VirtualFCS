VirtualFCS.Examples.ElectrochemicalComponents.CycleBatteryPack_Lumped

Example demonstrating constant-current constant-voltage cycling of a lumped Li-ion battery pack model.

Model Description and References

This example cycles a lumped Li-ion battery pack with a pulse current between -200 A and +200 A over 7200 s. The pack starts at SOC = 0.99 and is evaluated with BMS voltage limits of 40 V and 54 V.

The lumped formulation represents the summed pack behavior without individual cell states. Use the composite-pack example for cell-level behavior such as cell balancing.
