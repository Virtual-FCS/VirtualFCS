VirtualFCS.Fluid.Examples.PumpElectricDCTest

Simple model to test the PumpElectricDC model

Model Description and References

This test drives an electric DC pump with a trapezoidal control beginning at 50 s. The signal has amplitude 1, rise and fall times of 100 s, width 100 s, and a 400 s period.

The water circuit uses five parallel channels, a 1 m by 0.003 m inlet pipe, a 0.2 m by 0.01 m downstream pipe, and a 60 V nominal battery supply with 58 V to 62 V limits.
