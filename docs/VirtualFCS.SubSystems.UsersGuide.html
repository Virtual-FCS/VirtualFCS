User information for the SubSystems sub-library

The subsystem models encapsulate the main balance-of-plant functions around a fuel-cell stack.

Selection guide

  • Use SubSystemAir for cathode air supply and pressure control.
  • Use SubSystemHydrogen for tank, pressure regulation, recirculation, and purge.
  • Use SubSystemCooling for stack coolant circulation and reservoir-temperature control.
  • Use FuelCellSubSystems when all three lines should be exposed through one balance-of-plant interface.

Subsystem controllers are tuned examples, not universal operating maps. Recheck nominal flow, pressure, temperature, and controller parameters when changing the stack, medium, manifold volume, or operating range.
