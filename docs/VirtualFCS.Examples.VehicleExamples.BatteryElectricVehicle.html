VirtualFCS.Examples.VehicleExamples.BatteryElectricVehicle

Model Description and References

This example drives the Mirai vehicle profile with the NEDC drive cycle and a battery-electric powertrain. The battery starts at SOC = 0.9 with 200 Ah capacity, nominal voltage 320 V, and operating limits of 300 V to 360 V.

Vehicle velocity from the drive cycle determines the traction power demand.
