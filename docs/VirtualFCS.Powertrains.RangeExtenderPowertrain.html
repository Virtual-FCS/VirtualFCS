VirtualFCS.Powertrains.RangeExtenderPowertrain

Model Description and References

Battery-electric powertrain augmented with a hydrogen fuel-cell range extender. The fuel-cell system supplies the battery bus through a controlled DC converter. Battery measurements feed the replaceable energy-management system, whose power request is converted to a current command using the measured fuel-cell voltage. The fuel-cell air subsystem uses CompressorSimple and a one-cell membrane Humidifier. The converter flow-delay output starts at 1 A to avoid a zero-flow compressor initialization. In this assembly, the converter's FC-named pins connect to the battery and its Bus-named pins connect to the fuel-cell system.
