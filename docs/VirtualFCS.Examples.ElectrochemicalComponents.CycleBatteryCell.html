VirtualFCS.Examples.ElectrochemicalComponents.CycleBatteryCell

Example demonstrating constant-current constant-voltage cycling of a Li-ion battery cell.

Model Description and References

This example applies a symmetric pulse current to one Li-ion cell for a 7200 s charge-discharge cycle. The current alternates between -2.6 A and +2.6 A with a 3600 s half-period, starting from SOC = 0.

The cell has a 2.6 Ah capacity, 0.08 kg mass, and is cooled to 298.15 K. The BMS limits the cell voltage during the charge and discharge portions.
